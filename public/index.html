How a swap works

From sign-up to handing over the item, in three steps.

  1. 1

    Register

    Sign up with your FNU student email and confirm the 6-digit code we send you.

  2. 2

    List or browse

    Post an item with a photo and a price in FJD, or search for what you need.

  3. 3

    Chat and meet

    Message the seller, reserve the item and collect it on campus.
